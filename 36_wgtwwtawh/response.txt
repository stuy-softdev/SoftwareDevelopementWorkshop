Notes on the Telephone Launch:
- Jobs practiced for five days before his presentation and there was still always
a problem. Your demo might not be perfect, but practicing will make it as close to perfect as possible.
- Grignon felt anxious because Jobs would blame him for any problem that occurred.
All devos should take accountability for their own mistakes and help each other.
- Jobs made a big gamble by presenting a new kind of phone that barely worked. Although
it ended up being a success, the lesson is make sure what your demoing actually works.
- Jobs may not have had his priorities straight: He was very focused on the look of the presentation,
but wasn't focused on the issues with actually using the phone. It's important to maintain a
proper balance between presenting something and working on the function on what you're presenting.
- Jobs presentation was successful because he demonstrated all important features. The look
of the phone, using it to call someone and leave a voice mail, sending an email, typing, photos, etc.
